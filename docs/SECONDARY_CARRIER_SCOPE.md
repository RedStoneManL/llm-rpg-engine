# Secondary presentation carriers

The real repair candidate correctly created and handed over the wood and knife, but its untracked NPC cloth bundle triggered an unrelated custody repair. The old incidental-prop contract deliberately blocked turns with explicit item effects. This increment adds a separate, narrowly supported `secondary_carrier` role; it does not rename the bundle “background-only” or create a carrier Object from prose.

An extraction may propose a positive untracked prop held by an existing, already-present NPC as a presentation carrier for separately tracked contents. Its exact object phrase and explanation remain model interpretations. The only extra references are admitted content Object IDs and existing physical transition indices, avoiding a new claim-ID dependency graph.

The host checks that:

- Resource resolution is authenticated `none`; quest, promise and current return-commitment contexts remain blocked.
- Every typed item effect, Object creation, Object fact subject or Object relation endpoint is covered by the supported content set. An unknown or unadmitted member makes the exception ineligible without exposing its identity.
- The holder is a non-player existing Person, present before the turn and at an explicit checkpoint.
- Each referenced transition actually hands that content from this NPC to the actor. The same existing predicate evaluator proves NPC custody at every checkpoint from the carrier anchor through pre-handoff, then actor custody after handoff.
- The carrier is not bound to an existing/candidate Object or a literal match to its admitted identifier/name. All other custody, handoff, source-origin and effect checks still run independently.

Success means `out_of_scope`, not supported canonical carrier custody, existence, legal ownership or permission. No carrier effect is manufactured. The old background-only branch keeps its original restrictions. A mixed turn with unrelated item effects may remain conservatively ineligible because the entire typed footprint must be covered.

The model must not assign this role to a container the player requests, manipulates or inspects; a transferred/damaged/consumed carrier; evidence; access/concealment; a measured quantity; or an instrument producing a task result. Role, alias resolution, the causal relationship to contents and completeness of extraction remain probabilistic. The host does not prove those semantic exclusions by matching words. Uncertain cases retain bounded repair/rejection.

The schema change is explicit (`semantic_commit_v12`). References remain stable under eligible prose-only interpretation reuse because all non-prose packet inputs must remain identical. Source identities, transitions, or policies changing invalidates reuse and approval. Verification freezes the recorded Author candidate and uses fresh audits plus labelled constructed protection cases; it does not redraw the Author until a favorable story appears.
